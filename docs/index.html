SpatialLab Support

Place your first track

Open a project, choose Import tracks and select audio in Files. Listen on headphones. In Space, drag a track around the listener, then press Play. Export tracks from your DAW with a common start time to keep them aligned.

Use SpatialLab FX in GarageBand

Select an audio track, open Plug-ins & EQ, tap Edit and choose an empty slot. In Audio Unit Extensions, select SpatialLab FX. Use Draw path to trace a route and select its length in bars.

Save a mix or stems

In Export, choose Full mix or Stems, select WAV or MP3, and tap Render and save. Pick a destination in Files. If you cancel, Save previous render lets you choose a destination again.
