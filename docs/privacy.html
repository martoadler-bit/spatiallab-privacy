Azimora Field Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Azimora Field and Azimora Field FX process audio on your device. They do not send usage analytics, first-open notifications, advertising identifiers, audio or project data to the developer.

Audio and projects

Imported audio and standalone projects are stored in the app’s local storage. When you export, you choose the destination in Files. A cloud storage provider you select handles those files under its own privacy policy. In AUv3 hosts, plug-in settings can be stored with the host project.

Device motion

When you enable Follow device, Azimora Field uses orientation on the device to adjust the listening direction during playback.

Support requests

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to include to respond to your request. Questions about privacy or requests to delete support correspondence can be sent to martoadler@gmail.com.
